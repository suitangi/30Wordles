30 Wordles

30 Wordle games in 30 days.

A challenge to design a Wordle variant game every day for a month. Feel free to play around and hope you get to enjoy playing some of these as much as I enjoyed designing them.

Day 1 Bundle Each guess bundles a letter into the next. Day 2 Subtle Every guess scores its closeness to the answer out of 100. Day 3 Wobble Each guess wobbles, four letter guesses, five letter answer. Day 4 Toggle Five toggles hide a pattern — score 5/5 to win. Day 5 Meddle Two secret words, one board — each tile shows the better clue. Day 6 Crumble Tiles drop and score. Green survive, yellows cancel and grey crumbles. Day 7 Bubble Letters blow into bubbles. Strays pop, neighbors connect into a chain. Day 8 Swivel Two wordles at right angle share guesses and clues. Day 9 Stifle Every letter you play is stifled for the next guess. Day 10 Hustle Unlimited guesses, hustle for a faster score. Day 11 Castle Clues breach the walls from the outside in.
