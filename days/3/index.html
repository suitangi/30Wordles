Day 3

Wobble

Each guess wobbles, four letter guesses, five letter answer, eight guesses.

Rules

  • Each guess is 4 letters and wobble: odd guesses cover columns 1–4, even guesses cover columns 2–5, alternating with every guess.
  • Eight guesses, the 8th guess is full 5 letters to guess the answer.
  • At any point, type full 5 letters to submit the answer, but beware: this is the final guess!.
