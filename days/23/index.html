Mingle

Tiles never show their true colors — their outlines score the neighbors.

Rules

  • Every guess scores like normal wordle, but the clues stay hidden. Each tile's outline only scores its neighbors: 1 per adjacent yellow, 2 per adjacent green.
  • Max 4 points per mid-word letter, 2 points max at the ends.
  • 9 guesses. 5 greens to win.
