Day 24

Quartile

Four columns, five-letter guesses — the board wraps.

Rules

  • The answer is a 4-letter word. Guesses are 5-letter words that fill the 4-column board and wrap to the next row — each guess starts one column right of the last.
  • Normal wordle colors. Win by spelling the answer across a row — as one guess's run of letters, or the end of one guess meeting the front of the next.
  • Infinite guesses.
