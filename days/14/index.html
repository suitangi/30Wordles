Day 14

Couple

Your guesses are coupled to each other: each guess's last letter is locked in as the next guess's first.

Rules

  • The last letter of each guess is locked in as the first letter of the next one.
  • The coupled letter is locked in for you and scores like any other letter.
  • Six guesses. Five greens win as always.
