Day 9

Stifle

A normal wordle with one twist: every letter you play is stifled for the next guess.

Rules

  • Guess the five-letter word in six tries. Green, yellow and gray clues all behave as usual.
  • Every letter you submit is stifled for one guess. It returns the guess after.
