Day 15

Muddle

One word on a honeycomb — rows of fours and fives. Some letters sit between two columns and their clues muddle.

Rules

  • Seven rows of hexagons. Guesses alternate to match — five-letter word, then four-letter word...
  • All 7 rows score the same hidden 5-letter word. A four-letter tile sits between two columns: it goes green if its letter matches either neighbor, yellow if the letter is elsewhere in the word.
  • 7 guesses. 5 greens to win, just like normal wordle.
