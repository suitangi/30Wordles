Day 20

Scramble

Your guesses are scrambled in its own way.

Rules

  • Every row scrambles letters its own way — the number on each tile is the typed slot that tile takes.
  • Type in the space above the board, and see a preview of the scrambling. Enter your guess and it gets scrambled. 6 guesses.
  • 5 greens to win. Typing the answer itself always wins — no need to try to scramble into the answer.
