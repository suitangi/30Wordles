Fiddle

Every guess keeps 3 letters of the one before. Fiddle your way there.

Rules

  • The first guess is free. Every guess after must use at least 3 letters from the guess before it.
  • The counter on the right counts the letters you still owe — it checks off as you type them. Shared letters wear the steel outline. Doubled letters count per copy.
  • 8 guesses. 5 greens to win, just like normal wordle.
