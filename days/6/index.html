Day 6

Crumble

A Wordle that stacks. Words drop down the well, grey tiles crumble to dust, and two yellows meeting vertically cancel each other out.

Rules

  • Guess the five-letter word. Your answer drops in like tiles in Tetris.
  • Landing tiles score like Wordle. Grey crumbles to dust. A yellow on top of another yellow cancels out. Green tiles stay.
  • Win when the answer is fully horizontal in one line on the pile. Unlimited guesses.
