Day 30

Normal wordle — but clues have a cost. Gamble your clue budget wisely.

Rules

  • You hold 10 clues for the whole game. Set how many to spend on the side of your guess: grays show for free, greens and yellows each spend one. Once the row's budget runs out, everything after goes colorless, grays included.
  • Spent clues never come back, even if there were none in the guess — that's the gamble.
  • Unlimited guesses. Guess the word to win (regardless of budget). Fewer guesses and more leftover clues is the score.
