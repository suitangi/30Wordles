Day 25

Dividle

Every guess is cut in two — each side only clues its own half of the answer.

Rules

  • Normal wordle, but every guess carries a division line — slide it with the triangles or the ← → keys.
  • The cut divides your guess and the answer at the same spot; each side scores only against its own counterpart, and letters never clue across the line. Guess DO|NUT against POUND: the d grays — “do” never sees the d of “und”.
  • 7 guesses. 5 greens to win, just like normal wordle.
