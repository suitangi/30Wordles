Findle

A wordle word search

Rules

  • The 10×10 grid hides words in all 8 directions — across, down, backwards, diagonally. One is the answer.
  • Type any word you can read in the grid: at 5 letters it lights up on the board. 6 guesses.
  • Each tile grades itself: green sits on the answer; yellow lies on the target's row, column or diagonal; and gray misses it entirely.
