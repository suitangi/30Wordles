Day 11

Castle

The clues are walled. Breach the word from the outside in — a green at an open tile drops the walls beside it, and only five greens take the castle.

Rules

  • Unlimited guesses. Tiles 1 and 5 are open from the start; the inner tiles are walled and stay colorless.
  • A green on an open tile breaches the walls beside it, unlocking those tiles.
  • Walled tiles give no clues — even a perfect guess only breaches the open walls.
