Day 8

Swivel

Two wordles at right angles share their letters. Every guess scores across one board and down the other — swivel the board and the shared clues swap sides.

Rules

  • Two wordles intersected perpendicularly. The main board has 6 guesses; the horizontal board goes sideways with 3 extra guesses.
  • Type on whichever board is upright. Main guesses 2 – 6 also fill the horizontal board's first five columns, so those 25 shared letters carry main clues upright and horizontal clues swiveled.
  • Find both words to win. Got the main word before 6 guesses? Use the remaining word slots to find clues for the swiveled wordle.
  • Use the swivel button to change views at any time.
