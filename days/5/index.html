Day 5

Meddle

Two secret words tangle on one board — every tile shows the better clue of the two.

Rules

  • Two five-letter words in one board. Unlimited guesses.
  • Each tile shows the better of its two clues: Green > Yellow > Gray (Orange > Blue > Gray in colorblind mode)
  • Once a word is answerd, it drops out, and the clues turn to the word that's left. The game ends when both words are found.
