Day 7

Bubble

Every letter you play blows into a bubble. Letters in the answer stay afloat, neighbours connect into one long bubble — and the rest pop.

Rules

  • Guess the five-letter word in six tries. Each letter you submit blows up into a bubble.
  • Letters that aren't in the word pop. Letters in the word hold.
  • Letters that sit side by side in your guess and are neighbours in the word connect their bubbles into one long chain.
